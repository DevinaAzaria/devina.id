# devina.id Legacy WordPress Content Inventory

**Status:** Initial public-site audit  
**Audit started:** 3 September 2026  
**Source:** Legacy WordPress site at `devina.id`  
**Purpose:** Preserve the useful historical record of Devina's website before the portfolio cut-over.

> This inventory is a migration control document. An item marked `Drop` means it is not intended for the new public portfolio; it does **not** authorize deletion of the legacy WordPress database or media.

## Decision model

| Decision | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Portfolio Evidence** | Strong evidence of Devina's learning, interests, authorship, projects, or development. Curate prominently. |
| **Keep** | Worth preserving and publishing in the new site, usually in Journal/Archive. |
| **Archive** | Preserve historically, but do not feature prominently. |
| **Drop** | Low relevance to the future personal/academic portfolio. Preserve in the legacy backup/inventory until migration is closed. |
| **Review** | Authorship, provenance, context, or relevance needs verification before migration. |

## Legacy site areas discovered

The public site currently mixes several generations of purpose:

- personal/learning articles;
- science and technology explainers;
- coding-learning roadmaps;
- digital literacy and law;
- culture/fashion content;
- music/lyrics;
- SEO, marketing, business, and financial articles;
- food/recipe content;
- an old WooCommerce/TUNIQUE shop with multiple products;
- the current `Devina & Co.` business-oriented homepage.

This confirms that the migration should be **curated**, not a wholesale copy of the current WordPress information architecture.

## Migration principles

1. Preserve original publication dates for migrated historical writing.
2. Never present generic/editorial material as a personal achievement or original research without provenance verification.
3. Prefer evidence of process, learning, projects, reflection, culture, music, and academic development.
4. Consolidate duplicate or near-duplicate explanatory articles where appropriate while retaining legacy references in this inventory.
5. Keep old URLs in the inventory so redirects can be designed before WordPress is retired.
6. Media must be inventoried separately before `wp-content/uploads` is removed.
7. WordPress must remain untouched until content, media, redirects, and verification files have been accounted for.

## Cut-over gate

**Do not delete or clean the legacy WordPress installation yet.**

Cut-over is allowed only after:

- content inventory is substantially complete;
- retained media has been copied or otherwise preserved;
- redirect map is prepared;
- verification/SSL-related files are accounted for;
- a recoverable legacy backup/export exists or an explicit decision has been recorded not to retain one.
