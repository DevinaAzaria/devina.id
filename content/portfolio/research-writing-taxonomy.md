# Research & Writing Taxonomy

This file defines the editorial categories used on `devina.id`.

The purpose is accuracy, continuity, and traceable growth. A more prestigious label is never used when the underlying work does not support it.

## Core categories

### 1. Research Papers

Use when the work has a clear research question, method, evidence or data, analysis, conclusion, and references.

A school assignment, opinion piece, or explainer should not be upgraded to a research paper only because it is long or formal.

### 2. Technical Reports

Use for technical documentation of an experiment or engineering project.

A technical report should normally make the objective, Devina's role, setup or method, measurements or evidence, result, limitations, reflection, and next step visible.

### 3. Essays

Use for argument-driven or reflective writing that develops an idea, position, interpretation, or question.

An essay can use sources and evidence without being presented as a research paper.

### 4. Articles & Explainers

Use for accessible explanatory writing intended to teach or clarify a concept, tool, technology, or topic for a general reader.

Tutorial-like pieces can live here unless a separate tutorial collection becomes large enough to justify its own category.

### 5. Project, Lab & Field Notes

Use for process documentation, observations, experiments, project updates, field learning, failed attempts, revisions, and work-in-progress evidence.

This category intentionally includes **field notes** so culture, heritage, communication, and social-impact work can be documented without forcing them into a STEM-only format.

### 6. Reflections

Use when the main purpose is to document what changed in understanding, skill, judgment, decision-making, or future direction after an experience.

Reflections should point to the underlying project, event, evidence, or output when available.

## What is not a core category

- **Journal** is treated as an archive or historical URL structure, not a writing type.
- **Review / Commentary** can be added later if enough authentic work accumulates to justify it.
- **Tutorial / Guide** currently sits under Articles & Explainers.
- **Learning Notes** currently sit under Project, Lab & Field Notes when tied to an active project or learning process.

Avoid creating empty public category pages merely to make the portfolio look larger. The main Research & Writing landing page can show an honest “not yet published” state until work exists.

## Authorship and assistance

For every published work, distinguish among:

- work Devina can personally explain and defend;
- technical or editorial assistance;
- mentoring, supervision, or collaboration.

AI, parent, mentor, or DevinaHQ assistance must not be presented as independent work by Devina.

## Portfolio evidence fields

Where appropriate, each substantial work should make these fields traceable:

**Objective → Devina's Role → Process → Output → Evidence → Measurable Impact → Reflection → Next Step**

Not every short article needs all fields on the public page, but the underlying portfolio record should preserve them when relevant.


## SEO and publication standard

All new public writing should follow [SEO Writing Standard](./seo-writing-standard.md). The SEO rules support discovery and clarity; they do not override the authenticity and evidence rules in this taxonomy.
